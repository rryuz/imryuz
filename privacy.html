Privacy Policy

imRyuZ uploader · Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who uses this app

imRyuZ uploader is a personal tool. Its only user is the owner of the imRyuZ YouTube channel. It is not available to the public.

What data it accesses

With the owner's authorization, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload and schedule videos to the owner's own channel (scope youtube.upload) and to read basic information and statistics about the channel's own videos (scope youtube.readonly).

How data is stored

The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's own computer. Video statistics read from YouTube are used only to review the channel's performance. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Other people's data

The app does not access, collect or store data belonging to any other Google or YouTube user.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.

Google and YouTube policies

By using this app you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
